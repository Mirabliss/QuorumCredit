# Video Tutorial Script: "How to Vouch for a Borrower"

**Duration:** 5-10 minutes  
**Target Audience:** Non-technical community members, potential vouchers  
**Platform:** YouTube, community website  

---

## Scene 1: Introduction (0:00 - 0:30)

**Visuals:** QuorumCredit logo animation, friendly interface mockup

**Script:**

> "Hello! Welcome to QuorumCredit. My name is [Name], and today I'm going to show you how to become a voucher — someone who backs a borrower's loan and earns rewards for helping your community."

> "In traditional lending, you need mountains of collateral. With QuorumCredit, your **trust network is your collateral**. When you vouch for someone, you're saying 'I believe in this person.' And if they repay on time, you earn a 2% yield on your stake. It's that simple."

**On-screen text:** "Vouch. Trust. Earn. Repeat."

## Scene 2: Setup — Getting Your Wallet Ready (0:30 - 1:30)

**Visuals:** 
- Screen recording of Stellar wallet setup (Lobstr or Albedo recommended)
- Clear cursor highlighting key buttons
- Show account balance

**Script:**

> "First, you'll need a Stellar wallet. I'm using [Lobstr/Albedo], but any Stellar-enabled wallet works."

> "Step 1: Download the wallet app from [app store/browser extension]."

> "Step 2: Create a new account or import your existing one. You'll get a Stellar public address — this is your identity on the blockchain."

> "Step 3: Get some XLM to stake. You can buy XLM on any major exchange like Kraken, Binance, or Coinbase. For this tutorial, I'll use testnet XLM which is free."

**On-screen callout:** "Save your secret key in a safe place. Never share it with anyone!"

---

## Scene 3: Understanding Vouch Mechanics (1:30 - 3:00)

**Visuals:**
- Animated diagram showing: Borrower → Loan Request → Vouchers → Stake → Loan Funded
- Simple number examples on screen

**Script:**

> "Here's how vouching works. Let's say Maria wants to borrow 100 XLM to start a small business."

> "She creates a loan request on QuorumCredit, asking for 100 XLM and 5 vouchers minimum."

> "You and four of your friends see Maria's request. You each decide to vouch for her by staking 25 XLM. That's your 'skin in the game' — you're putting your money where your mouth is."

> "Once all 5 vouchers have staked their money, the loan is funded. Maria receives her 100 XLM and has 30 days to repay it."

> "Here's the exciting part: if Maria repays on time, each of you gets your 25 XLM back **plus a 2% yield** — that's 0.5 XLM extra. Free money for being a good judge of character!"

**On-screen graphic:**
```
Your stake:        25 XLM
Yield (2%):        +0.5 XLM
Your return:       25.5 XLM
Profit:            0.5 XLM
```

---

## Scene 4: The Risk — What Happens if the Borrower Defaults (3:00 - 4:00)

**Visuals:**
- Animated "X" mark over defaulted loan
- Simple math showing slash calculation

**Script:**

> "Now, what if Maria doesn't repay by day 30? That's where the 'slash' comes in."

> "If the loan defaults, each voucher loses **50% of their stake**. So from your 25 XLM, you'd lose 12.5 XLM."

> "This sounds harsh, but it's crucial. It means you should **only vouch for borrowers you genuinely trust**. You're not just earning yield — you're making a real commitment."

> "The slash goes into the protocol's reserve pool, which is used to pay yields to successful loans and protect the system from collapse."

**On-screen graphic:**
```
Default scenario:
Your stake:        25 XLM
Slashed (50%):     -12.5 XLM
Remaining:         12.5 XLM
Loss:              12.5 XLM ❌
```

---

## Scene 5: Walkthrough — Finding a Borrower (4:00 - 5:30)

**Visuals:**
- Screen recording of QuorumCredit dashboard
- Cursor hovering over loan requests
- Zoom-in on borrower profile

**Script:**

> "Let's walk through finding a borrower to vouch for. Open the QuorumCredit dashboard."

> "You'll see a list of pending loan requests. Each shows the borrower's name, amount needed, reason for the loan, and how many vouchers they still need."

> "Click on a loan to see the borrower's profile. Here you might find:"
>   - Their on-chain history (previous loans, repayment record)
>   - A short bio or message
>   - Common vouchers (friends who already vouched)
>   - The interest rate (fixed at 0% for borrowers)

> "Take a moment to evaluate. Ask yourself:"
>   - Do I know this person or trust their community?
>   - Is the loan amount reasonable?
>   - Do other trusted vouchers already back this loan?
>   - What's the stated purpose? (Emergencies are lower risk than speculative ventures)

> "Once you've decided, click 'Vouch' and select your stake amount. It must be between the protocol minimum and maximum."

**On-screen text callout:** "Not sure? Start with your friends. Vouch for people you actually know!"

---

## Scene 6: Staking Your XLM (5:30 - 6:30)

**Visuals:**
- Screen recording of vouch confirmation dialog
- Wallet confirmation flow
- Clear step-by-step buttons

**Script:**

> "Now you'll approve the vouching transaction. Here's what happens:"

> "Step 1: Click 'Vouch for this loan' and enter your stake amount."

> "Step 2: Review the details. Make sure the amount is right and the loan details are what you expect."

> "Step 3: Your wallet will ask you to confirm. This is a blockchain transaction, so there's a small fee (typically 1 stroop, which is 0.00000001 XLM — almost free)."

> "Step 4: Click 'Confirm' and wait. Your stake is now locked and public on the blockchain."

> "That's it! Your XLM is now staked as collateral for this loan. You can see it in your 'My Vouches' section."

**On-screen graphic:** Show transaction hash and confirmation

---

## Scene 7: Yield Calculation Deep Dive (6:30 - 7:30)

**Visuals:**
- Calculator animation
- Step-by-step math shown on screen

**Script:**

> "Let's talk about how yield is calculated, because it's fair and transparent."

> "Your yield depends on two things: **how much you staked** and **how long the loan is active**."

> "Here's the formula: Yield = (Your Stake × 2%) ÷ (Time to Repayment)"

> "Example: You staked 20 XLM on a 100 XLM loan. The loan is repaid after 15 days."

> "Your yield = 20 XLM × 2% = 0.4 XLM maximum."

> "So you get your 20 XLM back plus 0.4 XLM in profit."

> "Early repayment? You get your full yield. Late repayment? The contract calculates pro-rata yield based on actual time."

> "And remember: the borrower pays **zero interest**. All yield comes from a protocol reserve, making loans ultra-affordable."

**On-screen formula:**
```
Yield per Voucher = (Loan Amount × 2%) ÷ (Total Vouchers)
Your return = Your Stake + (Yield per Voucher)
```

## Scene 8: Withdrawal and Claiming Yield (7:30 - 8:30)

**Visuals:**
- Screen recording of 'My Vouches' dashboard
- Click flow for claiming yield
- Wallet confirmation

**Script:**

> "When the loan is repaid, you can withdraw your stake and yield anytime. Here's how:"

> "Open your 'My Vouches' section. You'll see all active and completed vouches."

> "For repaid loans, you'll see a green 'Claim Yield' button. Click it."

> "Your wallet confirms the transaction — again, nearly free (1-100 stroops fee)."

> "Within seconds, your stake plus yield appears back in your wallet. You're done!"

> "What if you want to withdraw before the loan is repaid? You can reduce your stake, but be careful — if you drop below the protocol minimum and the borrower defaults, you're unprotected."

**On-screen callout:** "Checking your balance: 1 XLM = 10,000,000 stroops. Your wallet might show stroops. Just divide by 10 million to get XLM."

---

## Scene 9: Safety Tips and Best Practices (8:30 - 9:15)

**Visuals:**
- Animated checklist
- Red flags highlighted
- Green flags highlighted

**Script:**

> "Before you start vouching, here are critical safety tips:"

> "✅ DO: Vouch for people you know or trust implicitly."
> "✅ DO: Diversify. Don't stake everything on one borrower."
> "✅ DO: Check the borrower's history. How have they repaid previous loans?"
> "✅ DO: Start small. Learn the system with low stakes first."

> "❌ DON'T: Vouch for strangers just because the yield looks high."
> "❌ DON'T: Stake more than you can afford to lose (50% slash hurts)."
> "❌ DON'T: Ignore red flags. Unknown borrowers, vague purposes, or no voucher history = high risk."
> "❌ DON'T: Ever share your secret key. QuorumCredit will never ask for it."

> "Remember: This is **real money**. Act like a bank manager making a loan decision, because that's exactly what you are."
